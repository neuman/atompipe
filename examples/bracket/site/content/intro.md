# A shelf bracket for a pump

This bracket holds a small pump on a shelf. It mounts on an outside wall with two bolts. The arm is 60 mm long and 7 mm thick, and it carries 15 N at its tip.

The person prints it in PETG on a printer with a 220 mm bed. Each board below says what the bracket must do. An evaluator, a test on a built part or a person settles each claim.

At 7 mm, the tip sags 0.70 mm against a limit of 0.5 mm, so C1 fails. The bracket is not ready to print.
