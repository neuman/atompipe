# A bracket for a pump on an outside wall

This bracket holds a small pump on a shelf. The shelf is on an outside wall, and the pump runs all day. The bracket is for a person who prints their own parts at home.

## The choice of PETG

The person asked to print the bracket in PETG on their own 220 mm printer (R4). Three other options lost.

- PLA creeps under a load that stays on. A PLA bracket can sag over months, and no static check sees it.
- ABS has no values in the model, so no evaluator here can check it.
- A machined aluminium bracket is a part that the person must buy. The person asked to print the part themselves.

## The arm

The arm is 60 mm long and 7 mm thick. It carries 15 N at its tip. The bending stress and the bolt stress stay inside their limits (C2, C3).

## Where the design is now

At 7 mm, the tip sags 0.70 mm against a limit of 0.5 mm, so C1 fails. A thicker arm is the cheap fix. A printed part must also hang outside through two winters and then carry the load again (C5). The bracket is not ready to print.
