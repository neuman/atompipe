The arm must carry the pump with no crack and no sag that a person can see.

## Sag at the tip

At 7 mm thick, the tip sags 0.70 mm under 15 N. The limit is 0.5 mm, so C1 fails by 40%. The sag goes as one over the cube of the thickness. A thicker arm is the cheap fix.

## Stress

The bending stress uses 0.245 of the allowed stress (C2). The bolts bear 0.195 MPa against an allowed 15 MPa (C3). Both evaluators read the same allowed stress: the yield divided by the safety factor of 2.

## The load

C6 says that the load is static and on the centre line of the arm. No owner records it yet. The sag claim rests on it, because the deflection evaluator reads the load.
